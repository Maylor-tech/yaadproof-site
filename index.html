Questions

Are you a builder?

No. We never do the construction and we never take a cut of materials or labour. That's what keeps us on your side.

Who visits my house?

A trained YaadProof site coordinator who lives in the area. Every visit follows the same checklist and photo rules.

Which parishes do you cover?

We're starting in Portland. Tell us where your house is and we'll tell you honestly whether we can reach it.

What if the work fails a check?

We tell you the same day, with photos and the fix needed. Labour for that stage stays on hold until a re-check passes.

I don't have a contractor yet. Can you help?

Yes. The Stalled House Assessment includes three quotes from contractors we've vetted. You choose; we check their work.
