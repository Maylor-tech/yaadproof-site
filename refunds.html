Cancellations and refunds

Last updated 20 November 2026

Site Check and Stalled House Assessment

  • More than 48 hours before the visit: cancel or reschedule for free, with a full refund if you cancel.
  • Less than 48 hours before: we'll reschedule once for free. After that, or once our coordinator has travelled to the site, the fee isn't refundable.
  • If we can't get in because of a locked gate, missing permission or similar, the visit counts as done. We'll offer a new date at the normal price.
  • If we have to cancel, you choose a new date or a full refund.

Roof Guard

  • The fee is paid in two halves: half when you book, half when Stage 3 passes.
  • If you stop before Stage 3, the second half isn't charged. The first half isn't refundable once we've delivered your quantity list and quotes.

Build Guard and Home Watch (monthly)

  • Cancel any time before your next monthly payment. There are no refunds for part of a month.

Money paid to contractors and suppliers

You pay contractors and hardware stores directly, so we can't refund those payments. Any refund for their work or materials is between you and them.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the way you paid, within 10 business days of approval.
