Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

YaadProof is a trade name of VelocityMaid LLC, a Vermont, USA company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or use our services, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Property details: where your house is, its condition, your drawings and anything you tell us about the job.
  • Photos and video of your property taken during our visits.
  • Payment records for fees you pay us. Card payments are handled by our payment provider; we don't see or store full card numbers.
  • Basic website data such as pages visited, collected by our website host.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a price.
  • To carry out visits, write your reports and keep track of your project.
  • To bill you and keep the records the law requires.
  • To improve our service.

We don't sell your information, and we don't send marketing messages unless you ask for them.

Who sees it

  • Our site coordinator in Jamaica, who needs your property details to do the visit.
  • Contractors or suppliers, only with your permission (for example, to get quotes for your job).
  • Service providers we use to run the business, such as our email, website and payment providers.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

Photos of your property

Photos are taken for your report. We'll only use them in our marketing if you say yes, and we never publish your street address. Our photo stamp shows only the community name.

How long we keep it

We keep project records for up to three years after your last job, or longer if the law requires it, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we have to keep it by law. Just email us.

Keeping it safe

We limit access to the people who need it and use reputable providers. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care.
